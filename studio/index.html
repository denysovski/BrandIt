              How to have better marketing

              A short method you can finish this week. Write the answers down as you read, then use them every time you open BrandIt.

              Write the sentence you stand for

              Fill in the blanks and keep it on one line. If it does not fit, you are saying too much.

              For these people who want this, we do this, so that this changes for them.

              For runners who train before work, we make light jackets that dry in an hour, so that they can go straight to the office.

              Then pick three words for how a stranger should describe you after one post, for example calm, precise, warm. Every post has to fit all three.

              Describe the people you want

              Answer these about one real customer, not about “everyone”.

              • What do they already follow and buy?
              • What are they tired of seeing?
              • What would make them stop scrolling?
              • What do they need to believe before they buy?

              Fix your kit once

              Decide these one time and save them in Brands, so every post starts from the same place.

              Fonts
              Two: one for headlines, one for small text.
              Colours
              A background, a text colour and one accent. The accent marks one thing per post.
              Logo
              Small, same corner, every time.
              Margins
              The same distance from the edge on every format.

              Choose the photo first

              People see the picture before they read a word. A good one does half the selling.

              • One subject, nothing competing with it.
              • Empty space where the headline will sit.
              • The same kind of light across the whole feed.
              • Your own product in real use, shot or licensed by you.

              Squint at the post. If you cannot tell what it is about, change the photo before you change the text.

              Give each post one job

              Decide the job before you open a template, then pick the look that does it best.

              Show it
              The product, big and clear. Try Bold photo.
              Prove it
              A number, a result, a before and after. Try Data & stats.
              Explain it
              Answer the question people ask most. Try Editorial.
              Invite
              One clear action and a date. Try Professional.
              Be you
              The people and the work behind it. Try Minimal.

              Keep a weekly rhythm

              Three posts a week is enough if they come every week. Plan the week on Monday in ten minutes.

              • PlanWrite the job and one headline under eight words for each post.
              • MakeUse two or three templates and reuse them all week.
              • ReviewSave the posts to one project and look at them side by side.
              • ShipExport every format and schedule them.

              After four weeks, look at saves and shares, not likes. Make more of what people saved.

              Check before you post

              • The headline reads in one second on a phone.
              • There is one clear thing to look at first.
              • It uses your fonts, colours and logo.
              • It makes sense without the caption.
              • It fits your three words.

              You are probably ready to post. Treat this as the beginning, not the finish. Keep learning from other brands and platforms, watch what your posts do, and above all keep listening to your customers: they decide what your brand means.
